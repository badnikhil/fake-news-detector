## Reading

Most frequent category over all 310 errors: **style overlap (wire-style fake / tabloid-style real)** (154 = 50%). Missed fakes (fake→real) are the user-facing danger; they are listed first. Near-duplicates with the opposite label are corpus label noise (WELFake merges four sources that sometimes carry the same story with different labels) — no classifier can be right on both copies. Wire-style fakes and tabloid-style reals show that the model reads register, not veracity — the reason the app's verdict comes from retrieved evidence and the classifier only adjusts confidence (master §14).
