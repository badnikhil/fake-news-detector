# Cross-dataset results — WELFake∖ISOT ↔ ISOT (`docs/results/cross_dataset.md`)

Generated by `python -m src.eval.cross_dataset` (part of `make eval-models`); raw numbers in `docs/results/train_logs/cross_dataset.json`. **Test splits**, macro-F1. Models = TF-IDF + LR / LinearSVC with their WELFake GridSearchCV winners (`docs/results/gridsearch_{lr,svm}.csv`), refitted on each training domain.

## Why WELFake∖ISOT and not WELFake

WELFake was assembled from Kaggle + McIntire + Reuters/**ISOT** + BuzzFeed, so **37,542 of the 37,567 processed ISOT articles (99.93%) occur verbatim in processed WELFake** (hash join on SHA-1 of `re.sub(r"\W+", " ", text.lower())`), covering 37,542 / 60,799 WELFake rows. Removing them leaves 23,257 rows; a further 25 are MinHash near-duplicates (Jaccard ≥ 0.9) of an ISOT article and are dropped too → **WELFake∖ISOT = 23,232 rows** (per split/label: {'test/fake': 985, 'test/real': 1327, 'train/fake': 7881, 'train/real': 10751, 'val/fake': 975, 'val/real': 1313}). Ids: `data/splits/welfake_minus_isot.csv` (`src.eval.cross_dataset.load_welfake_minus_isot()`). Plain WELFake → ISOT is in-domain and is never reported as cross-dataset.

## Leakage audit — artefact-only classifier on ISOT

Logistic regression on the 26 artefact-pattern counts only (`src.preprocess.artefacts.artefact_only_accuracy`, 5-fold CV accuracy, seed 42) — no words, only datelines / `(Reuters)` / credits / captions / URL debris:

| Text | CV accuracy | ± std |
|---|---|---|
| raw ISOT (as downloaded, 44,898 rows) | **0.9973** | 0.0006 |
| processed ISOT (artefacts stripped, 37,567 rows) | 0.5568 | 0.0001 |

On raw ISOT a classifier that never sees a content word is almost perfect: ISOT's real class is all Reuters wire copy ("WASHINGTON (Reuters) –" datelines) and its fake class comes from a handful of sites with fixed trailers, so a raw in-domain ~99 % measures *source style*, not truth. After stripping, the artefact-only classifier falls to about the majority-class rate. What survives in-domain (≈ 0.98–0.99 on processed ISOT) is still house style of disjoint outlets — the cross-domain cells above are the honest estimate of what a text classifier knows, and the reason the verdict comes from evidence (master §14).
